# Contact credits: release gate and rollback

Production remains at protected Netlify deploy `6ad679e83291e75b856113ca` until explicit approval. This change has not created live Stripe prices, applied the migration to production, or deployed. Do not use `supabase db push` for this release: the branch also contains a separate worker activation migration that must retain its own release decision.

## Verified product rules

- New one-time contact purchases: RD$100 for 1, RD$445 for 5, RD$890 for 10. No new unlimited passes. Spanish hosted Checkout checks each active DOP price, exact minor-unit amount, mode, and live/test environment before opening a session.
- Search, worker registration, availability, applications, and worker-initiated contact with a consenting opportunity owner remain free. Employer discovery and candidate review are free.
- A verified employer may re-open an already paid direct contact, a credit-funded contact, or a logged contact from a paid workspace term without another credit. Active paid workspace passes remain honored. A historical weekly/monthly pass can be linked to a verified account after independent support review; browser IDs or phone numbers alone are never proof.
- A PostgreSQL transaction serializes each employer's reveals, validates the stored phone, applies hourly/daily harvest limits, and spends one credit for a new worker. Refunds and disputes revoke unused credits and contact access funded by the affected pack. A partially refunded pack is suspended in full pending reconciliation. Won disputes restore access only after Stripe reports reinstated funds, and stale dispute events cannot revoke it again. Signed paid-webhook replay cannot re-credit a refunded order.
- The business support number is `12675160983` in the application and `netlify.toml`. Recovery and claim pages use the same source, so an old Netlify environment override cannot redirect those links.
- The private daily financial funnel includes paid credit packs, refunds, disputes, and authorized contact handoffs for reconciliation.

## Rollback

1. Immediately set the **new checkout** gate `CONTACT_CREDITS_CHECKOUT_ENABLED=false`; do not revoke paid read access or delete ledger rows. If a security defect affects contact reads, close the affected contact route at the application/edge until a verified fix is deployed. Keep worker browsing, applications, and availability available.
2. Restore protected Netlify deploy `6ad679e83291e75b856113ca` only after reviewing its contact behavior against the incident. The old artifact does not understand credit orders, so leave the additive ledger in place and route any paid credit customer to verified support until the fixed artifact is redeployed. Do not roll back the SQL by dropping financial tables.
3. Reconcile open Stripe sessions, signed events, refunds, disputes, and credits against the private ledger. If a webhook failed, replay the signed event after fixing the cause; never insert credits from a browser return. Re-enable checkout only after the issue is reproduced and the corrected build passes the same gate.
