## Rollback and recovery

Disable `CONTACT_CREDITS_CHECKOUT_ENABLED` first, preserving paid reads. If contact privacy is affected, close the affected server route until a verified fix is ready. Restore protected deploy `6ad679e83291e75b856113ca` only after checking its contact behavior against the incident. Leave the additive credit ledger and past purchases intact; the old artifact cannot understand new credit orders, so route affected paid customers to verified support. Reconcile open Stripe sessions, signed events, refunds, disputes, and credits before reopening checkout. Replay failed signed webhooks after fixing their cause; never grant credits from a browser return. Do not drop financial tables or mutate production customer rows to force rollback. Any restore of the production database would overwrite later orders and must be separately approved against a timestamped export and reconciliation plan.
